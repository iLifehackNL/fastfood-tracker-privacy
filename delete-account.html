Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your Fastfood Tracker account and all associated personal data at any time.

METHOD 1

Delete directly within the app (Instant)

1. Open the Fastfood Tracker app on your device.

2. Navigate to Profile / Settings.

3. Under the Danger Zone / Account section, tap Delete Account.

4. Confirm the prompt. Your user record, logged visits, passport history, and tier lists will be permanently deleted immediately.

METHOD 2

Request deletion via email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, email us from your registered account address at:

support@fastfoodtracker.app

Subject: Delete My Account

All stored records associated with your email will be permanently wiped from our database within 48 hours.
